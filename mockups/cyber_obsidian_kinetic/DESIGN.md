## Brand & Style

The design system projects hyper-modern intelligence, spatial clarity, and rapid temporal optimization for high-velocity teams and autonomous scheduling. It targets tech-forward professionals, modern enterprise operators, and digital native founders who expect software to feel like precision avionics rather than legacy business software. 

The aesthetic is a calibrated hybrid of **Futuristic High-Contrast Dark UI** and **Refined Glassmorphism**. Spatial depth is created via deep obsidian layers, precision gradient borders, and focused kinetic light fields rather than heavy drop shadows. Interfaces should feel laser-machined, luminous, and computationally sharp, projecting an atmosphere of algorithmic certainty and seamless coordination.

## Colors

The palette relies on layered vacuum-black and space-navy foundations punctuated by focused photonic accents:

- **Surface Foundations**:
  - `Canvas Base`: `#090D16` (Deep Obsidian Void)
  - `Surface Tier 1`: `#0D1322` (Sub-surface layer for large structural panels)
  - `Surface Tier 2`: `#141C33` (Elevated cards, active calendar blocks, modals)
  - `Surface Overlay / Glass`: `rgba(20, 28, 51, 0.65)` with backdrop filters

- **Photonic Accents**:
  - `Primary (Cyber-Indigo)`: `#6366F1` — Used for active interaction zones, primary CTA fills, and algorithmic recommendations.
  - `Secondary (Electric Cyan/Teal)`: `#06B6D4` / `#22D3EE` — Used for real-time slot availability, timeline playheads, and success indicators.
  - `Tertiary (Ultraviolet)`: `#8B5CF6` — Used for AI optimization badges, secondary glows, and multi-participant scheduling overlaps.

- **Data & Signal Tokens**:
  - `Status Available`: `#22D3EE` (Neon Cyan)
  - `Status Conflict / Critical`: `#F43F5E` (Laser Crimson)
  - `Status Processing / AI`: `#A855F7` (Electric Orchid)

Avoid pure `#000000` flat fills; always anchor dark spaces with the subtle oceanic blue-cast of `#090D16` to retain depth. High-saturation accents must be isolated to 10–15% of the viewport to preserve visual hierarchy and avoid cognitive fatigue.

## Typography

Typography balances geometric humanism with technical monospaced precision:

- **Headlines (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Set with tight character tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and heavy weights (`700`, `800`) to evoke a sculpted, cinematic presence across time-zone and calendar metrics.
- **Body & Controls (`Inter`)**: Neutral, highly legible at micro-sizes, optimized for high dynamic range against dark obsidian surfaces.
- **Technical Readouts & Badges (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Micro-labels, UTC offsets, coordinates, and latency indicators utilize `micro-tech` in all-caps to reinforce the autonomous AI framework.

Always use tabular numbers (`tnum`) for numeric indicators, timeline hours, and capacity percentages to eliminate horizontal jitter during live updates.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout utilizes a responsive 12-column fluid grid on desktop (`1280px+`), collapsing into an 8-column layout on tablet (`768px - 1279px`), and a 4-column layout on mobile (`<768px`).

- **Base Rhythm**: Anchored to a strict 4px/8px sub-grid. Component padding, gaps, and line-heights are deterministic multiples of 4px.
- **Responsive Adaptations**:
  - **Desktop**: Three-pane cockpit architecture (Sidebar Navigation [64px or 240px expanded], Dynamic Schedule Canvas [flexible], Contextual Slot Intelligence Panel [380px fixed]).
  - **Tablet**: Intelligence panel collapses into a floating bottom sheet or contextual sliding drawer; timeline transitions to swipeable time-columns.
  - **Mobile**: Single-column vertical cascade with fixed bottom navigation bar and condensed slot intervals (`space-sm` internal padding).
- **Z-Index Layering**: Grid backplates (`0`), glass card containers (`10`), sticky scheduling headers (`20`), floating action controls (`30`), modal/diagnostic overlays (`50`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on diffused gray drop shadows. Instead, it employs three distinct techniques:

1. **Luminous Stacking (Surface Tiers)**:
   - `Canvas Base` (`#090D16`): The ultimate ground plane.
   - `Tier 1` (`#0D1322`): Secondary framing, side rails, structural panels.
   - `Tier 2` (`#141C33`): Cards, interactive time slots, calendar nodes.

2. **Chameleon Gradient Outlines**:
   - Elevated elements use `1px` translucent borders: `linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12) 0%, rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.25) 50%, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02) 100%)`.
   - Hover states boost the border gradient opacity to reveal sharp cyber-indigo and cyan edge highlights.

3. **Photonic Glows & Glassmorphism**:
   - `Subtle Float`: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.15)`.
   - `Active/Focus Neon Bloom`: `0 0 24px -2px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.35), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(34, 211, 238, 0.6)`.
   - `Glass Surfaces`: Backdrop blur of `16px` to `24px` combined with high-translucency background `rgba(20, 28, 51, 0.6)`.

## Shapes

The geometric language balances streamlined industrial edges with soft, ergonomic touchpoints:

- **Corner Radius Scaling**:
  - `Base (8px / 0.5rem)`: Interactive slot cards, inputs, dropdown items, calendar grid cells.
  - `Large (16px / 1rem)`: Floating dialogs, main workspace panels, flyout inspector sheets.
  - `Extra Large (24px / 1.5rem)`: Modal windows, hero prompt containers.
  - `Pill (9999px)`: Status tags, AI recommendation chips, primary filter toggles, profile action buttons.

Precision borders (`1px`) are mandatory on all shaped containers to isolate dark shapes from black backdrops without adding visual weight.
